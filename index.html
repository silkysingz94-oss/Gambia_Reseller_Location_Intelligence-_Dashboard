Methodology & interpretation
This dashboard provides exploratory spatial analysis of the 2023 business enterprise dataset. Business counts represent records in the loaded dataset, not necessarily a verified estimate of all operating enterprises. Map patterns depend on the coordinate reference system, completeness and positional accuracy of the source data. Heatmap intensity represents relative spatial concentration of mapped records.
